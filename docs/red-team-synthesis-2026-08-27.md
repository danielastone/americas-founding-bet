# Red-team synthesis — 2026-08-27

## Overall verdict

The repository is a disciplined **pre-conclusion research design**, not yet an empirical demonstration of America's founding fiscal-financial system.

Its strongest asset is governance: explicit falsification rules, careful accounting-state distinctions, preservation of negative findings, and increasingly honest admission controls. Its weakest asset is the one that matters most: an adequate source frame for testing the essential claim across 1789–1803.

The project currently has **zero admitted annual-equivalent periods against a minimum requirement of nine**. None of the seven registered claims has passed or failed. Three computed P1 controls are favorable but availability-conditioned and provisional.

## What is genuinely established

1. The repository distinguishes research design from conclusion.
2. The 1792 P1 arithmetic and Statement A controls reconcile exactly.
3. The checked FRASER 5631, 5636, and 5637 figures are numerically consistent with the official source images within the bounded spot-check.
4. Early customs assessments cannot be silently substituted for cash received into Treasury.
5. The 1797 Q4 control exposes a real liquidity question: debt-service cash exceeded current customs cash.
6. The project has recorded several negative source findings instead of converting them into favorable proxies.

These are methodological results. They do not establish the governing thesis.

## What is missing

### Essential

1. **Designed annual federal-account frame.** Complete, compatible receipts and financing denominators across the three precommitted windows.
2. **Compatible annual debt-service series.** Interest, principal, commissions, and foreign-fund treatment on the same period basis.
3. **Liquidity panel.** Customs remittances, bond maturities, Treasury balances, payment due dates, temporary borrowing, and delays.
4. **Louisiana face-to-settlement reconciliation.** Authorization, obligations, issue, market value, net proceeds, fees, exchange, and existing-service continuity.
5. **Attributable verification.** Named or otherwise attributable independent source-image checks tied to observation records.

### Important only after the essential gates survive

- security prices and comparable European-market controls;
- one preselected successful and one adverse merchant transaction;
- First Bank operational records;
- port and commodity generalization.

## What is extra right now

The following material may be historically relevant, but it is not currently decision-relevant:

- broad state and city fiscal layers;
- household welfare and consumer-demand expansion;
- global commodity-market context beyond variables needed for explicit controls;
- lighthouse and revenue-cutter layers;
- broad diplomatic narratives not tied to the endpoint counterfactual;
- large-scale BIBFRAME or publication integration;
- additional convenient FRASER-period extraction without a sampling frame.

The cost is not just time. Each additional layer creates the appearance of evidentiary depth while leaving the essential test unresolved.

## Conceptual correction

AFB-C001 is compound. “Customs dominance” and “supporting debt service” require separate decisions:

- P1 can establish revenue composition.
- P3 must establish timing, liquidity, and bridging.
- A burden ratio such as `D_c` cannot trace which dollars financed which payment.

The thesis must not use a P1 result as shorthand for P3 reliability.

## Stop/go plan

### Gate 1 — Federal source-frame feasibility

Resolve whether RG 217 central accounts, equivalent Treasury records, or a defensible triangulated alternative can supply compatible annual observations across the required windows.

**Go:** a documented source frame can plausibly meet the coverage rule without choosing periods by result or convenience.

**Stop/narrow:** the source frame cannot meet the rule at reasonable cost. Publish the source-survival and accounting problem, and narrow the thesis to bounded case periods.

### Gate 2 — P1 admission

Complete and independently verify enough annual denominators to apply the precommitted rule.

**Go:** P1 passes or yields a clearly bounded mixed result.

**Stop:** P1 fails. Abandon the governing customs-dominance thesis.

### Gate 3 — P3 liquidity

Construct timing evidence for the admitted P1 periods.

**Go:** customs realization and bridging support defensible reliability language.

**Narrow:** replace “dependable capacity” with “fragile, bridged, or episodic.”

### Gate 4 — P2 Louisiana endpoint

Reconcile the Purchase financing and test whether prior fiscal capacity materially explains it.

**Go:** retain Louisiana as the endpoint stress test.

**Stop/narrow:** treat Louisiana as an adjacent diplomatic-financial case.

Only after these gates should the project spend heavily on merchant, Bank, port, commodity, welfare, or publication layers.

## Immediate priority

The next task is not another source transcription. It is a **source-frame feasibility decision** with a bounded acquisition budget, explicit coverage targets, and a termination rule.

Until that decision is made, the repository should describe itself as an archival research design under feasibility testing—not as a supported thesis.
