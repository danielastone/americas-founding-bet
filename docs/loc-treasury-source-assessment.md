# LOC Treasury manuscript source assessment

## Collection

**United States Department of the Treasury Records**, MSS83125, Manuscript Division, Library of Congress. The collection contains 975 items in ten containers, twenty-nine oversize containers, and nine microfilm reels. It was assembled from multiple accessions and is explicitly described as an assortment of largely unrelated records.

Finding aid: https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/gdc/gdcfindingaidpdfs/ms014048/ms014048.pdf

## High-value discovery

The addition titled *U.S. Finance, tabular extracts of receipts and expenditures of the United States Treasury, 1784–1790* is available only on microfilm, shelf 24,495.

This is the only item in the collection description that directly promises receipts-and-expenditures tables covering the unresolved opening federal period. It is therefore P1-critical. It is not yet evidence for any amount.

Before extraction, the microfilm must answer:

1. Are 1789–1790 federal transactions distinguishable from Confederation-period entries?
2. Are dates transactional, posting dates, or reporting-period labels?
3. Are customs, tonnage, loans, balances, and other receipts separately classified?
4. Does “receipts” mean collector receipts, Register postings, or cash received by the Treasurer?
5. Are drawbacks and collection expenses grossed or netted?
6. Is the film independent of RG 217, or derived from the same books?

## Bounded supporting containers

- Box 1 and Oversize 2: appropriations, 1789–1795. This is authority evidence, not cash.
- Box 1 and Oversize 3: Treasury circulars beginning in 1789. These can define required procedures.
- Box 3: general correspondence, 1789–1802. Review only for named anomalies.
- Box 4: letterbook, 1789–1874. Establish its early coverage before transcription.
- Box 1 and Oversize 1: certificates for the loan of August 4, 1790. Financing only.
- Box 2 and Oversize 8: Virginia commissioner-of-loans stock abstract, 1799–1803. Debt and financing only.

The remaining early-looking material is dominated by Revolutionary military accounts, pensions, diplomatic expenditures, or later subjects and does not solve the customs-cash problem.

## Access route

The researcher has direct LOC access, so interlibrary loan is unnecessary. Request the reel through the Manuscript Reading Room, LM 101, using the exact identifiers **MSS83125** and **microfilm shelf 24,495**. LOC states that readers must present a verified Reader Identification Card; staff or building access should not be assumed to replace reading-room registration.

Before visiting, confirm that shelf 24,495 is retrievable in LM 101 and whether advance notice is required. The collection finding aid warns that some manuscript material is stored off-site. Use `docs/requests/loc-mss83125-microfilm-retrieval.md` as the bounded retrieval and inspection sheet.

## Decision

Do not request all ten containers. The immediate target is shelf 24,495 through direct Manuscript Reading Room access. Boxes 1–4 become targeted follow-ups only if the film or RG 217 daybook exposes a specific unresolved definition, collector, port, or transaction.
