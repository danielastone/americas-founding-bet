# FRASER 5640: 1801 annual P1 reconstruction

## Decision

The December 1801 Treasury report does not support a complete annual `C_s`, `C_u`, or `D_c` ratio by itself. It contains two exact annual Treasury-cash receipt figures, but not the complete compatible receipt denominator or full-year cash debt-service numerator.

The report explicitly says that the Treasury accounts were settled through September 30, 1801. The missing annual account therefore existed administratively; it is not reproduced in this FRASER item.

## Admissible annual components

For the year ending September 30, 1801:

| Component | Amount | Accounting state |
|---|---:|---|
| Merchandise and tonnage duties | $10,126,213.92 | receipts into Treasury |
| All internal revenues | $919,719.16 | receipts into Treasury |

The internal-revenue figure is the image-corrected value. The degraded text layer reads $910,219.16, but the page image reads $919,719.16.

These two components total $11,045,933.08. This is not the complete non-loan denominator.

## Missing or incompatible receipt components

| Item in report | Reported amount | Why it cannot complete the annual denominator |
|---|---:|---|
| Public-land sales through Oct. 31 | $834,887 total sales; $248,461 paid | cumulative sales-office measure, one month beyond fiscal year, Treasury custody not established |
| Postage | $50,000 | prospective annual estimate |
| Bank dividends | $70,040 | rate-based revenue estimate, not period cash receipt |
| Direct tax paid into Treasury by Dec. 1 | $1,245,000 | cumulative-to-date measure ending two months after fiscal year |
| Public-vessel sales | $275,767.73 sold; $86,412.83 paid by Sept. 30 | cash paid is period-bounded at the endpoint but start date and Treasury custody are not explicit |
| Incidental receipts | no annual total | omitted |
| Loan proceeds | no compatible annual statement | absence from narrative is not proof of zero |

The report's $10.6 million “permanent revenues” total is an estimate for future years, not realized 1801 cash.

## Debt-service incompatibility

The report gives a full-year decline in unredeemed principal of $2,279,317.31, but that is a stock reconciliation rather than a complete cash-payment account. It also says more than $800,000 had been remitted to Holland for obligations falling due in 1802.

For April 1 through September 30, 1801, the report precisely identifies principal payments of $1,187,016.78:

- sinking-fund reimbursement: $129,048.83;
- temporary bank-loan principal: $500,000.00;
- Dutch-debt principal after interest and commissions: $536,685.29;
- public-debt evidence accepted for lands: $21,282.66.

This six-month principal account cannot be combined with the annual customs denominator to compute `D_c`. Full-year interest paid is not reported in a compatible cash statement.

## Falsification consequence

The known annual Treasury receipts already show customs far larger than internal revenue, but missing land, postal, dividend, incidental, asset-sale, direct-tax, and financing flows prevent a valid denominator. Computing a ratio from only the two known categories would mechanically favor the thesis.

The annual observation remains blocked. The exact next source target is the settled Register/Treasurer account for the year ending September 30, 1801, or the separately printed statement of receipts and expenditures referenced by Gallatin. The target must report all receipts, financing inflows, opening balances, and debt-service payments for the same period.
