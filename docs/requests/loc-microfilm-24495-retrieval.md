# LOC retrieval protocol — microfilm shelf 24,495

## Target

- Collection: U.S. Department of the Treasury Records, MSS83125 additions
- Item: *U.S. Finance, tabular extracts of receipts and expenditures of the United States Treasury, 1784–1790*
- Format/location: microfilm, shelf 24,495
- Research purpose: determine whether the item can supply or reconcile central federal receipt and expenditure accounts for 1789–1790

## Before viewing

Confirm:

- reader-card and reading-room requirements;
- whether advance retrieval is required;
- the correct microfilm service location;
- photography, scanning, and reproduction rules;
- whether a reel or frame list exists.

Do not request Boxes 1–4 during this reconnaissance.

## Capture order

1. Reel label, collection identifier, and any target/provenance frames.
2. Item title page or opening caption.
3. Compiler, office, creation date, and stated source books, if present.
4. Every heading defining receipt, expenditure, balance, or accounting period.
5. The complete sequence covering March 4, 1789 through December 31, 1790.
6. Totals, footnotes, corrections, continuation markers, and blank/gap indicators.
7. Closing frames identifying the end of the item.

Capture surrounding frames whenever a table begins or ends mid-frame.

## Stop conditions

End the reconnaissance without broad transcription if:

- the cited item is absent;
- no 1789–1790 material is present;
- periods or custody states cannot be identified;
- the reel is only a later summary without recoverable construction;
- image quality prevents reliable reading.

A negative inspection is a valid feasibility result.

## After viewing

- Save frame-level notes and images under a stable local naming convention.
- Add one inspection record to the archival request or source-frame log.
- Keep all amounts in staging until provenance, accounting state, and independent verification are resolved.
- Compare provenance with T964 before treating the films as independent evidence.
