## First-pass inspection

Do not begin full transcription. Capture only enough evidence to determine whether the source is usable:

1. reel title, provenance statement, introductory frames, and any source citation;
2. complete table headings and column definitions;
3. every page or frame covering March 4, 1789 through December 31, 1790;
4. period labels and whether years are calendar, fiscal, or posting periods;
5. lines for customs, impost, tonnage, drawbacks, collection expense, loans, balances, and miscellaneous receipts;
6. any language distinguishing accrued, collected, remitted, deposited, or received into Treasury;
7. totals and footnotes;
8. evidence that identifies whether these are copies, extracts, or original office records.

Record image filenames as:

`LOC_MSS83125_24495_frame-####_YYYYMMDD.jpg`

If the reader exports TIFF, retain TIFF as the preservation master and make JPG or PNG derivatives for repository review.

## Stop rule

Stop after the first-pass capture. Do not transcribe the entire reel until the accounting basis and independence from RG 217 are established.

## Evidence outcome codes

- `cash_compatible`: explicitly received into Treasury or posted to a central cash account;
- `collector_cash_only`: collected locally but transfer to Treasury unproved;
- `accrual_only`: duties accrued or arising;
- `financing`: loans, certificates, or asset proceeds;
- `stock_or_balance`: cash or account balance at a date;
- `basis_unresolved`: terminology insufficient;
- `derivative_source`: copied or compiled from another identifiable record.
