# NARA Archives I reference inquiry: RG 217 early federal accounts

**Status:** Responded 2026-08-31  
**Recipient:** archives1reference@nara.gov  
**NARA responder:** Russell Hill, Archives 1 Reference Branch  
**NARA request:** RR1R-26-86199  
**Subject:** RG 217 series identifiers and access for Treasury receipts and expenditures, 1789–1803

## Inquiry sent 2026-08-26

I am researching the federal fiscal accounts of the United States from 1789 through 1803 and am trying to identify the originating Treasury records behind published early financial statements.

NARA’s Guide to Federal Records describes the following under Record Group 217, Office of the Register of the Treasury, Receipts and Expenditures Division:

- daybooks, 1789–1894;
- abstracts of receipts and expenditures of the United States, 1789–1889, with an index beginning in 1791;
- journals, 1776–1799;
- blotters, 1782–1810;
- ledgers, 1776–1923.

The request asked for current catalog identifiers and Inventory 14 entries, container lists, facility location, M1014/M1015/T964 mapping, roll lists, digitization status, reproduction options, and representative volumes for 1789–1790, 1797–1798, 1800–1801, and 1802–1803. It also asked whether the records separately identify customs cash, internal duties/direct taxes, land/incidental receipts, financing proceeds, Treasury balances, debt service, and accounting-period adjustments.

## NARA response — 2026-08-31

Archives I provided the following catalog routes as a courtesy and noted that Abstracts and Ledgers are divided among several series:

| Record series | NARA route supplied |
| --- | --- |
| Daybooks, 1789–1894 | National Archives Catalog ID 2517180 |
| Abstracts of receipts and expenditures of the United States, 1789–1889 | Catalog search for `abstracts`, RG 217, series/collection level |
| Journals, 1776–1799 | National Archives Catalog ID 2517189 |
| Blotters, 1782–1810 | National Archives Catalog IDs 16614774 and 2635545 |
| Ledgers, 1776–1923 | Catalog search for `ledgers`, RG 217, series level |

NARA stated that it generally provides information sufficient for researchers to begin their own research rather than conducting the research itself. It invited an onsite research-room visit or use of an independent researcher for examination and copying.

## What the response resolves

- Confirms actionable catalog routes for all five requested RG 217 record families.
- Provides direct identifiers for Daybooks, Journals, and two Blotter series.
- Confirms that Abstracts and Ledgers must be resolved across multiple series rather than treated as single catalog objects.
- Confirms that the next evidentiary step is item/container-level archival inspection, not another broad reference request.

## What remains unresolved

The response did **not** supply:

1. Inventory 14 entry numbers;
2. volume, box, or container lists and inclusive dates;
3. specific facility/research-room location beyond Archives I context;
4. whether M1014, M1015, or T964 reproduce these exact series;
5. roll lists or descriptive pamphlets;
6. whether relevant volumes are digitized through NARA or a partner;
7. off-site reproduction limits/order procedure;
8. representative volume/sample images for the four requested periods;
9. confirmation that the records contain the analytical fields needed for the federal fiscal reconciliation.

## Research-control interpretation

This response is **source-discovery evidence only**. It does not validate any historical amount or accounting classification. None of the identified series should be promoted to E1 evidence until the relevant archival item is identified and inspected.

The efficient next move is to resolve the supplied catalog records and their child/related series into an item-level reconnaissance list. A second generic reference inquiry would add little unless it asks a narrowly targeted question that cannot be answered from the catalog or onsite finding aids.

No purchase, copying order, independent-researcher engagement, or research visit has been authorized by this log entry.
