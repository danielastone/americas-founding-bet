# NARA Archives I reference inquiry: RG 217 early federal accounts

**Status:** Prepared; not sent  
**Recipient:** archives1reference@nara.gov  
**Subject:** RG 217 series identifiers and access for Treasury receipts and expenditures, 1789–1803

## Draft inquiry

I am researching the federal fiscal accounts of the United States from 1789 through 1803 and am trying to identify the originating Treasury records behind published early financial statements.

NARA’s Guide to Federal Records describes the following under Record Group 217, Office of the Register of the Treasury, Receipts and Expenditures Division:

- daybooks, 1789–1894;
- abstracts of receipts and expenditures of the United States, 1789–1889, with an index beginning in 1791;
- journals, 1776–1799;
- blotters, 1782–1810;
- ledgers, 1776–1923.

Could you please provide, for the records covering 1789–1803:

1. the current National Archives Catalog identifiers and Inventory 14 entry numbers;
2. the volume, box, or other container list, including inclusive dates for each relevant unit;
3. the facility and research-room location of the records;
4. whether Microfilm Publications M1014, M1015, or T964 reproduce any of these specific daybooks, abstracts, journals, blotters, indexes, or ledgers;
5. any roll lists or descriptive pamphlets for applicable microfilm;
6. whether any relevant volumes are digitized in the National Archives Catalog or through a partner;
7. available off-site reproduction options, practical page or volume limits, and current ordering procedure.

The immediate research question is whether the central records separately identify:

- customs cash received;
- internal duties and direct taxes;
- land and incidental receipts;
- loan or other financing proceeds;
- opening and closing Treasury balances;
- interest payments and principal redemption;
- accounting periods and adjustments.

If feasible, identifying one representative volume or sample image for 1789–1790, 1797–1798, 1800–1801, and 1802–1803 would help determine whether a broader reproduction request is warranted.

I am not requesting a general search of all early Treasury holdings. The initial scope is limited to the central Receipts and Expenditures Division series listed above.

Thank you.
