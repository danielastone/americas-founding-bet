# P1 federal source-frame feasibility gate

## Decision question

Can the project identify a non-opportunistic source frame capable of supplying complete, accounting-compatible annual federal receipts and debt-service evidence across 1789–1803?

This gate tests feasibility, not the customs-dominance thesis. No historical amount is admitted merely because a series appears promising.

## Coverage target

A feasible frame must plausibly support at least the precommitted P1 minimum:

| Window | Required annual-equivalent periods |
|---|---:|
| 1789–1792 | 3 |
| 1793–1797 | 3 |
| 1798–1803 | 3 |
| **Total** | **9** |

The periods must arise from a declared source frame. They may not be selected because their surviving statements are legible, complete, or favorable.

## Minimum field test

For each candidate annual account, determine whether the source identifies or permits reconciliation of:

1. accounting period;
2. receiving account or custody point;
3. customs cash received;
4. internal duties and direct taxes;
5. land, postage, fees, repayments, and other operating receipts;
6. domestic and foreign borrowing proceeds;
7. asset-sale and Bank-stock proceeds;
8. opening and closing Treasury balances;
9. interest, principal, commissions, and exchange;
10. refunds, drawbacks, collection costs, and material adjustments.

A source may pass through linked books or statements if the links and accounting bases are explicit. Heterogeneous proxies do not become a frame merely because they can be placed in the same spreadsheet.

## Bounded acquisition plan

### Work package A — LOC shelf 24,495 reconnaissance

- One reading-room retrieval.
- Initial inspection cap: four hours.
- Capture only provenance frames, table headings, period labels, account classifications, totals, and the complete 1789–1790 table sequence.
- Do not transcribe individual transactions or inspect unrelated MSS83125 boxes.
- Record image/frame references and whether photography or reproduction is permitted.
- Stop immediately if the reel does not contain the cited item or the tables lack identifiable accounting periods and custody states.

### Work package B — Pending NARA response

- Do not duplicate the RG 217 inquiry before the logged response window ends.
- On receipt, extract series identifiers, entry numbers, container/volume dates, location, microfilm overlap, and reproduction constraints.
- Request or inspect one representative unit for each target interval: 1789–1790, 1797–1798, 1800–1801, and 1802–1803.
- No paid reproduction order or archival visit is authorized by this plan.

### Work package C — Frame assessment

Create a row for every surviving annual unit—not only useful or legible units—and score the ten minimum fields as present, linked, absent, or unknown. Document gaps and classification changes.

Initial research cap before a decision: one LOC reconnaissance session plus analysis of the first substantive NARA response. Any broader copying order, travel, or large transcription batch requires a new decision.

## Decision rule

### Go — full P1 frame

Proceed to acquisition only if the identified series and bounded supplements plausibly provide:

- the 3/3/3 annual coverage distribution;
- compatible or explicitly reconcilable accounting states;
- complete receipt and financing denominators;
- compatible debt-service measures;
- a frame defined independently of observed ratios.

### Narrow — bounded-period study

Narrow the thesis if the records support internally complete accounts for fewer periods but cannot meet the 3/3/3 rule. Predefine the surviving frame and report it as a case-period study. Do not retain a 1789–1803 persistence claim.

### Stop — source-feasibility failure

Stop P1 as designed if central records are inaccessible, discontinuous, or classification-incompatible and the gaps would require heterogeneous estimates or available-period selection. Preserve the failure as a finding about early federal accounting and archival survival.
